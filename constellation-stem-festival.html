About this Constellation

The Maryland STEM Festival is a statewide celebration that brings hands-on STEM experiences to communities across Maryland through events hosted by schools, libraries, museums, universities, businesses, and community organizations.

As an MSE3 Constellation, the Festival becomes a shared platform that any Partner can host, join, sponsor, or help lead, and a front door that connects families and students to the rest of Maryland’s STEM ecosystem, including MDIC inventors, robotics teams, and -Ship Yard opportunities.

Four Ways to Take Part

Host, participate, sponsor, or lead

  • Host a FestivalSchools and communities that want to host a STEM Festival event in their own community receive planning guidance, promotion, and connections to exhibiting Partners.
  • ParticipatePartners that want to exhibit, present, or run activities at an existing, established Festival event are matched with host sites.
  • Sponsor or Request a FestivalIndustry partners can sponsor Festival events, or request a new Festival in a specific community or location where they want to build talent and visibility.
  • Lead the Festival’s Next ChapterThe Maryland STEM Festival is entering a leadership transition. MSE3 invites individuals and organizations to join a new stewardship group, made up of MSE3 Partners and leadership, that will guide the Festival forward.

Growth targets are measured as a percentage of current levels, so the first goal is to set the benchmark.

Measurable outcomes

  • Establish a benchmark of current Festival events, event participants, and event sponsors by March 2027.
  • A stewardship group of at least seven members, representing at least four MSE3 sectors, formed with an adopted governance charter by March 2027.
  • 15 percent growth in the number of Festival events over the benchmark in 2027, and 30 percent by 2028.
  • 20 percent growth in event participants over the benchmark by 2028.
  • 25 percent growth in event sponsors over the benchmark by 2028, including new community Festivals launched through Industry requests.
  • An increase of 10 percentage points in the share of Festival events held in Not-Yet-Reached communities, with events in all five MSE3 regions.
  • Every Festival event connects attendees to at least one -Ship Yard opportunity or MSE3 Constellation, with results reported annually against the benchmark.
