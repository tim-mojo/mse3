Robotics is a Sport in Maryland

About this Constellation

Robotics is a Sport in Maryland elevates robotics competitions as a core STEM engagement pathway, positioning them alongside athletics in visibility, support, and student recognition. This initiative builds a sustainable statewide competition pyramid, from regional and county championships to multiple Maryland Robotics State Championships.

With the expansion of Fibonacci Centers, it ensures equitable access by reducing reliance on schools alone to provide space, coaches, and resources. The Hub’s proposed Physical AI / Robotics Challenge adds an AI-enabled track built on existing VEX and FIRST formats (see the Maryland AI Coordination Hub).

Air, Land, and Sea

Target: 5 percent growth in student participation in each area

  • Air (Drones)Drone competitions paired with an educational and vocational component of training and licensing, such as FAA Remote Pilot certification, that connects students directly to new -Ships. Programs include the Drone Soccer League and local events hosted by Escape Velocity Academy (escapevelocityacademy.com).
  • LandGround robotics programs including VEX Robotics, FIRST, and the FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC).
  • SeaUnderwater robotics through SeaPerch (seaperch.org).

Local Leagues and -Ships

Championships that start and end in Maryland

An increase in local County and Cross-County Championships that start and end in Maryland, with no focus on National Championships. These local leagues are funded by Industry partners and lead to an increase in local scholar-Ships and Summer intern-Ships available to students participating in Air, Land, and Sea programs.

Measurable outcomes

  • 5 percent growth in student participation across each of the three major areas of Maryland robotics competition: Air, Land, and Sea.
  • Increase in the number of Industry-funded County and Cross-County Championships held in Maryland each season.
  • Increase in local scholar-Ships and Summer intern-Ships offered to students participating in these programs.
  • Increase in students earning drone training credentials and licenses that connect to -Ship opportunities.

Start a team, host a championship, or sponsor a local league.
