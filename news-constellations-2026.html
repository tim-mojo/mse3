Introducing the 2026 MSE3 Constellations

MSE3 News · October 2026

Six cross-sector Constellations, each with a clear purpose, clear ways for Partners to engage, and measurable outcomes.

MSE3 has published version 2.0 of its Constellations for 2026: six focused, cross-sector working groups that unite Industry, Academia, Out-of-School Time, Government, Military, Community Service Organizations, Museums and Informal Science Institutions, and Nonprofits around one shared goal each.

What changed in version 2.0

Future Ready Maryland: Accelerate AI is now organized as three components, each with its own complete set of measurable outcomes: A1, AI in the Workforce; A2, AI in K-20 Education; and A3, Ignite AI: Communities and Community Organizations. The Maryland AI Coordination Hub is the umbrella over all three, and its progress is measured through them.

A1 now leads with employer-partnered AI upskilling for current employees, delivered in sector cohorts through Maryland community colleges, starting with the state’s lighthouse industries.
