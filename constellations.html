Constellations 2026

Making Maryland STEM-rich, STEM-ready, and STEM-relevant.

Building Maryland’s Future-Ready Workforce, One Constellation at a Time.

What is a Constellation?

Constellations are the delivery engine of the Maryland STEM Education to Employment Ecosystem (MSE3). Each Constellation is a focused, cross-sector working group that unites Industry, Academia, Out-of-School Time, Government, Military, Community Service Organizations, Museums and Informal Science Institutions, and Nonprofits around one shared goal. Every Constellation has a clear purpose, clear ways for Partners to engage, and measurable outcomes that let Maryland track progress toward a STEM-literate, STEM-skilled workforce, especially for the Not-Yet-Reached.

The six 2026 Constellations

Constellation A

Future Ready Maryland: Accelerate AI

One statewide AI readiness Constellation, from the workforce to the classroom to the community.

Brings MSE3’s AI work together around the full continuum of AI readiness — literacy, proficiency, fluency, and competency — for Marylanders at every stage of life.

A1 · AI in the WorkforceA2 · AI in K-20 EducationA3 · Ignite AIUmbrella · Maryland AI Coordination Hub
Who it serves

Employers and their employees, students, educators, and faculty (K-20), community organizations, nonprofits, museums, small businesses, local government

Headline outcome
  • A1: Employer-partnered AI upskilling for current employees through community colleges
  • A2: More school systems with a formal AI strategy, toward 25%+; growth in college and university AI curriculum
  • A3: Hour of AI in all 24 jurisdictions; more community organizations offering and using AI
  • Umbrella: Statewide AI Coordination Hub connecting all three
Explore Accelerate AI →
Constellation B

Future Ready Maryland: The -Ship Yard

Launching Maryland’s Next Generation of Talent. Building Maryland’s Future, One -Ship at a Time.

Maryland’s launchpad for career-connected learning: internships, apprenticeships, externships, scholarships, mentorships, Co-Op (Work Study), and career opportunities in STEM.

Who it serves

Employers, higher education, STEM partners, students, job seekers

Headline outcome
  • 10% increase in STEM-relevant -Ships
  • Gap reduction and new career type growth, measured against a baseline
Explore The -Ship Yard →
Constellation C

Robotics is a Sport in Maryland

Robotics, a stepping stone where everyone can go Pro!

Elevates robotics competitions across Air, Land, and Sea alongside athletics, building a sustainable statewide competition pyramid with equitable access through Fibonacci Centers.

Who it serves

Schools, Fibonacci Centers, teams, coaches, Industry sponsors, Community-based Service organizations

Headline outcome
  • 5% growth in Air, Land, and Sea participation; more local -Ships
Explore Robotics is a Sport in Maryland →
Constellation D

Future Ready Maryland: Maryland Invention Convention (MDIC)

A Signature Constellation of Future Ready Maryland. Where Maryland’s K-12 students become inventors.

Maryland’s annual K-12 statewide invention competition — an affiliate of The Henry Ford’s Invention Convention Worldwide, powered by UMBC’s Sherman Center.

Who it serves

K-12 schools, after school programs, libraries, judges, sponsors

Headline outcome
  • Student submission and participation targets for Year 2 and Year 5, to be defined by MDIC
Explore Maryland Invention Convention →
Constellation E

Maryland STEM Festival

Bringing hands-on STEM to every Maryland community.

A statewide celebration and shared platform that any Partner can host, join, sponsor, or help lead — and a front door to the rest of Maryland’s STEM ecosystem.

Who it serves

Host communities, exhibiting partners, industry sponsors, future Festival leaders

Headline outcome
  • Baseline established, then percentage growth in events, participants, and sponsors
Explore Maryland STEM Festival →
Constellation F

STEMsphere Community of Practice

We work together to make a Future Ready Maryland.

A convening of STEM-centric organizations in the DMV, held five times a year, where Partners from every Constellation compare notes, avoid duplication, and find new collaborators.

Who it serves

STEM-centric organizations, school districts, private schools, and colleges

Headline outcome
  • All 24 school districts and every region represented
Explore STEMsphere CoP →
