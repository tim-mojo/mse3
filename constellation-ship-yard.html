Future Ready Maryland: The -Ship Yard

About this Constellation

The -Ship Yard is Maryland’s launchpad for career-connected learning, where -Ships are built and launched: internships, apprenticeships, externships, scholarships, mentorships, Co-Op (Work Study), and career opportunities in STEM. This Constellation increases the number of businesses offering work experiences, the number of STEM partners and higher education institutions offering the education and training component, and the number of Marylanders participating in those opportunities.

Industry Advisory Board Design Session

Setting the working model

In 2027, MSE3 convenes an Industry Advisory Board Design Session at UMBC with Industry, Academic, and OST/STEM partners to define shared priorities and a working model for this Constellation. The Design Session formally establishes the Industry Advisory Board, and its outcomes shape the inaugural Maryland STEM Workforce Summit in 2027.

Measurable outcomes

  • A 10 percent increase in STEM-relevant internships, apprenticeships, scholarships, and work/study opportunities in Maryland.
  • Industry Advisory Board Design Session completed, formally establishing the Industry Advisory Board, with priorities adopted for the -Ship Yard model.
  • Inaugural Maryland STEM Workforce Summit held.
  • Gap reduction: an increase in the percentage of -Ships filled by students and job seekers from Not-Yet-Reached communities.
  • New career and job type growth: an increase in the number of new occupation types offering -Ships each year, including emerging fields such as AI, drones, and advanced manufacturing.
A note on baselining. The gap reduction percentage requires a baseline before growth can be measured. MSE3 will work with Industry and Academic Partners to establish a baseline of current -Ships and who fills them, beginning with data collected at the 2027 Industry Advisory Board Design Session and the inaugural 2027 Maryland STEM Workforce Summit.
