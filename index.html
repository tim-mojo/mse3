WHAT IS MSE3

An ecosystem model built on real‑world career connections.

Guided by Industry and Academic Advisory Boards, MSE3 strengthens Maryland’s STEM talent pipeline — cultivating innovation, equity, and sustainability while equipping Marylanders with the skills to solve tomorrow’s challenges.

HOW WE DO IT

The Constellation model

Through six signature Constellations, MSE3 empowers partners across the state to expand what’s possible in STEM.

A

Future Ready Maryland: Accelerate AI

A single, statewide AI-readiness Constellation uniting AI in Education, Ignite AI, and the Maryland AI Coordination Hub — from the classroom to the community to the workforce.

B

Future Ready Maryland: The -Ship Yard

Maryland’s launchpad for career-connected learning: internships, apprenticeships, externships, scholarships, mentorships, and co-ops.

C

Robotics is a Sport in Maryland

Elevating robotics competitions across Air, Land & Sea alongside athletics, building toward a statewide competition pyramid.
