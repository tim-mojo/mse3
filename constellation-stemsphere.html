Constellation F

STEMsphere Community of Practice

We work together to make a Future Ready Maryland.

At a glance
Who it serves

STEM-centric organizations, school districts, private schools, and colleges

Headline outcome
  • All 24 school districts and every region represented
5
sessions per year, with a summer break
24
Maryland school districts participating
5
Maryland regions, each with at least one college or university engaged

About this Constellation

STEMsphere CoP is a convening of STEM-centric organizations in the DMV, held five times a year with a summer break, to share programming, events, and workforce development opportunities such as internships, externships, apprenticeships, and scholarships.

It is where Partners from every Constellation compare notes, avoid duplication, and find new collaborators.

Measurable outcomes

  • Five sessions per year, with a summer break.
  • A Partner presentation at every session.
  • Participation by all 24 Maryland school districts.
  • An increase in private school participation of at least 10 schools or 5 programs, with participation from each of the five Maryland regions.
  • At least one college or university engaged from each of the five Maryland regions.
