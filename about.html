ABOUT US

Championing partnerships and innovation across Greater Maryland.

“To amplify STEM education, nurture talent, and create dynamic workforce pathways that meet the needs of Maryland for tomorrow’s challenges and opportunities.”

The ecosystem model

Industry leads. The whole pipeline follows.

MSE3 starts with what Maryland employers need, then connects academia, students, and the workforce around it, so the talent the ecosystem builds flows straight back into Maryland industry.

1
Industry
Employers set the agenda: the skills Maryland needs, the Constellations they fund, and the jobs they hire for.
2
Academia (K–20)
Schools, colleges, and universities align curriculum, credentials, and hands-on learning to those needs.
3
Students
Marylanders, especially the Not-Yet-Reached, gain skills and real-world experience through -Ships and Constellations.
4
Workforce
Graduates step into STEM careers across Maryland, and back into industry as its next hires and leaders.

And back to industry: the workforce the ecosystem trains becomes the employers, mentors, and sponsors who set the next agenda.

Meet STEMI

STEMI stands for STEM Innovation and Entrepreneurship.

Our Maryland crab is a reminder of what the ecosystem is for: turning Maryland’s STEM talent into new ideas, new businesses, and new careers, right here at home.
