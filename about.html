ABOUT US

Championing partnerships and innovation across Greater Maryland.

“To amplify STEM education, nurture talent, and create dynamic workforce pathways that meet the needs of Maryland for tomorrow’s challenges and opportunities.”

THE ECOSYSTEM MODEL

Where Academia, Industry, and Students meet.

MSE3 exists at the intersection of three groups — each strengthening the others to build Maryland’s STEM talent pipeline.

Academia (K–20)
Schools, colleges, and universities delivering curriculum, mentorship, and hands-on learning across the K–20 pipeline.
Industry & Sponsors
Employers and funders providing real-world problems, investment, and career-connected experiences.
Students → Workforce
Marylanders gaining the skills and experience that lead directly into STEM careers statewide.

Academia prepares talent, industry invests and hires, and students become Maryland’s next STEM workforce — feeding back into the ecosystem that trained them.
